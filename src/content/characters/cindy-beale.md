Cindy Beale (née Williams) first appeared in EastEnders in 1988 and became one of
its defining antagonists across the 1990s, her turbulent marriage to Ian Beale at
the centre of some of the show's biggest storylines. Written out in 1998, the
character was later said to have died. She returned to Albert Square in 2023.
