Michelle Collins is one of the few performers to have held major roles in both of
Britain's biggest soaps. She originated Cindy Beale in EastEnders in 1988, and
years later joined Coronation Street as Rovers Return landlady Stella Price. In
2023 she returned to EastEnders, reprising Cindy.
