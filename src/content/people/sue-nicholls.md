Sue Nicholls first appeared in Coronation Street as Audrey Potter in 1979,
becoming a regular in 1985, and has played the character — later Audrey Roberts —
ever since. As the Platt family's outspoken matriarch and hairdresser, she is
among the programme's most enduring figures.
