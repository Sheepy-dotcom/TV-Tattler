Jeff Hordley has been Cain Dingle since 2000, but the role hasn't been quite
unbroken: a departure in the late 2000s took the Dingles' enforcer off screen for
a spell before he returned to the village. For a character built on grudges and
loyalty in equal measure, an exit and comeback only deepened the mythology.
