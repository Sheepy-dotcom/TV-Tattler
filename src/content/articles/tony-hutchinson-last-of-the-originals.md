Hollyoaks has always been a soap of fast turnover, so its longest-serving
character is a rarity: **Nick Pickard** has played Tony Hutchinson without a break
since the very first episode in 1995. He is the last of the original cast still on
screen — a living link back to the show's beginnings.

His [reference page](/characters/tony-hutchinson/) has the timeline in full.
