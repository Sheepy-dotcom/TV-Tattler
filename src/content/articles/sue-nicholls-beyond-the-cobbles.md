Most viewers know Sue Nicholls as Audrey Roberts, the Coronation Street salon
owner she has played since 1979. But her television career stretches back well
before Weatherfield, taking in a run of much-loved British comedies and dramas
across the 1960s and 70s.

That longevity is part of what makes her Audrey so grounded: a performer entirely
at home on screen, bringing decades of craft to one of the Street's most beloved
matriarchs.
