Few EastEnders characters have had a life quite like Cindy Beale's. She arrived in
1988, spent a decade at the centre of Albert Square's most explosive storylines,
was written out at the end of the 1990s — and then, long after audiences had filed
her under "gone for good", came back.

## Who plays Cindy Beale?

Cindy has been played throughout by **Michelle Collins**, who originated the role
in 1988 and reprised it on the character's return. That continuity is unusual: many
long-gap soap returns involve a recast, but Cindy came back with her original
actor.

## The character in brief

Cindy began as the girl next door and quickly became one of the show's defining
antagonists, her on-off marriage to Ian Beale driving storyline after storyline.
Her exit at the end of the 1990s was written as a permanent one.
