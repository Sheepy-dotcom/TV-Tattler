Some soap characters are defined by drama; Dot Cotton was defined by decency — a
launderette matriarch whose faith, gossip and forgiveness anchored EastEnders for
a generation. She was played throughout by **June Brown**, from 1985 until 2020.

Dot's [reference page](/characters/dot-cotton/) records both of June Brown's stints
and the gap between them.
