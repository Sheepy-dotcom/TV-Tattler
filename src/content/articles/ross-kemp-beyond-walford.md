Plenty of soap actors move on to other drama; fewer reinvent themselves as
frontline reporters. After making his name as Grant Mitchell in EastEnders,
**Ross Kemp** built a substantial second career in documentaries — reporting from
gangs, war zones and prisons.

It's a reminder that our people pages aren't just soap CVs: an actor's page pulls
their roles from the portrayal records, and articles like this one sit alongside,
covering the rest of the career. See [Ross Kemp's page](/people/ross-kemp/).
