When Coronation Street opened its first episode on 9 December 1960, a young
schoolteacher named Ken Barlow was among the faces on screen. He is still there.

## The longest line in soap

**William Roache** has played Ken continuously since that first episode, a run
recognised by Guinness World Records as the longest by any television soap actor in
a single role. No recasts, no long absences written around another performer — one
actor, one part, for the show's entire life.

## A different shape from the Beales

Where EastEnders' Cindy Beale offers a story of exit and return, Ken is the
opposite: pure continuity. Put their timelines side by side and you can read two
completely different careers straight off the portrayal bars — which is exactly
what the reference pages are for.
