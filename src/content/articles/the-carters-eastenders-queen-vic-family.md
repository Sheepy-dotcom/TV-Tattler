Every era of EastEnders has a family that holds the Queen Victoria — and for the
2010s, it was the Carters. When landlord **Mick Carter** (Danny Dyer) and his wife
**Linda** (Kellie Bright) pulled their first pints in 2013, they brought a warmth
and a working-class romance that quickly made them the show's emotional centre.

Mick was the big-hearted governor: quick with a joke, fierce in defence of his
own, and — beneath the banter — carrying wounds that drove some of the soap's most
affecting storylines. Danny Dyer played him until a dramatic 2022 exit that left
Walford without one of its most beloved figures. Linda, meanwhile, has remained
the Vic's beating heart; Kellie Bright is the only actress to have played her.
