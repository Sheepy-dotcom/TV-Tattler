When the Slaters arrived in 2000 they brought the noise — and Albert Square has
never been the same. At the centre is **Kat** (Jessie Wallace), all leopard-print
and fierce devotion, and **Stacey** (Lacey Turner), whose life has carried some of
the show's biggest stories. **Jean** (Gillian Wright), Stacey's mother, anchors the
family with warmth and hard-won resilience.

Loud, loving and forever in each other's business, the Slaters are Walford family
drama at its most unfiltered.
