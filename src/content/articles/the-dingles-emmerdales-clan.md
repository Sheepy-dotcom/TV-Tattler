No family has shaped modern Emmerdale like the Dingles — a sprawling, scrapping,
fiercely loyal clan at the centre of the village. **Cain** (Jeff Hordley) and his
half-sister **Chas** (Lucy Pargeter) are two of its anchors.

Because the characters are linked as family, their reference pages cross-connect,
and each actor's stint reads straight off the portrayal timeline — see
[Cain](/characters/cain-dingle/) and [Chas](/characters/chas-dingle/).
