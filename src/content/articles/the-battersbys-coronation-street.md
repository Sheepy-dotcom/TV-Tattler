The Battersbys crashed into Coronation Street as the neighbours from hell and
gradually became part of its furniture. Sisters **Leanne** (Jane Danson) and
**Toyah** (Georgia Taylor) have grown from tearaway youngsters into two of the
Street's central figures, carrying decades of romance, business ventures and
heartbreak between them.

For all the chaos, the Battersbys endure on the same principle as the best soap
families: fierce, messy loyalty.
