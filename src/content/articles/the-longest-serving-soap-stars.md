British soap rewards staying power, and a handful of actors have made whole
careers inside one role. Coronation Street alone has two of the greats —
**William Roache** as Ken Barlow since 1960 and **Barbara Knox** as Rita since
1972 — while **Nick Pickard** has been Hollyoaks' Tony since 1995.

## Read it off the records

We don't keep a leaderboard by hand. Every one of those runs is stored as a
portrayal record on the character, and each actor's page reads their tenure
straight back out of it — so the timelines you see on
[Ken Barlow](/characters/ken-barlow/), [Rita Tanner](/characters/rita-tanner/) and
[Tony Hutchinson](/characters/tony-hutchinson/) are the source of truth, not a
list we maintain separately.
