If the Dingles are Emmerdale's beating heart, its marriages are the valves. **Cain**
(Jeff Hordley) and **Moira** (Natalie J. Robb) have run hot and cold across years of
farmyard drama, a partnership as combustible as it is enduring. **Chas** (Lucy
Pargeter) and the gentle vet **Paddy** (Dominic Brunt) have offered a softer, no
less turbulent counterpoint.

Between them, these two couples have carried a remarkable share of the show's
storylines — proof that in soap, a strong marriage is really a strong story engine.
