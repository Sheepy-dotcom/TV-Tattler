If Coronation Street has a defining quality, it's the strength of its women. **Rita
Tanner** (Barbara Knox) has been a Weatherfield institution for decades; **Audrey
Roberts** (Sue Nicholls) has ruled the salon since 1979; and **Gail Platt** (Helen
Worth) has survived more than most. Alongside them, **Sally Metcalfe** (Sally
Dynevor), **Eileen Grimshaw** (Sue Cleaver) and **Carla Connor** (Alison King)
carry the modern Street.

Between them they represent a remarkable depth of long service — and a good deal of
the show's spark.
