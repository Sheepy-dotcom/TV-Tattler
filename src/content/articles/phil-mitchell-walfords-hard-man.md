If the Beales are one half of EastEnders' backbone, the Mitchells are the other —
and Phil Mitchell is their centre of gravity. He arrived in 1990, played from the
start by **Steve McFadden**, and has never been recast.

## The constant of the Mitchell clan

Across three decades Phil has run the car lot, pulled pints at the Queen Vic, and
sat at the heart of the show's biggest feuds. Like Ian Beale, he's the kind of
single-actor, decades-long character our reference pages are built to track — see
his [portrayal record](/characters/phil-mitchell/) for the full run.
