Moving between Britain's two biggest soaps is rare — the two have long felt like
opposing camps. **Michelle Collins** has a foot in each: she made her name as
Cindy Beale in EastEnders, then took over the Rovers Return as Stella Price in
Coronation Street.

## Read it off the record

You don't have to take our word for the shape of that career — it falls straight
out of the portrayal data. Her [profile](/people/michelle-collins/) lists every
role and stint, pulled from the characters she has played across both shows.
