Hollyoaks has built much of its reputation on issue-led drama, and few characters
have carried more of it than Ste Hay. Played by **Kieron Richardson** since 2006,
Ste has been at the heart of some of the show's most prominent storylines.

Across nearly two decades he has been written through addiction, abusive
relationships, a far-right grooming arc and — in one of the show's most acclaimed
stories — an HIV diagnosis handled with unusual care and praised by sexual-health
charities. That mix of vulnerability and resilience is what has kept the character
central to Hollyoaks for so long.

Richardson's long, unbroken run in the role makes Ste one of the soap's defining
leads: the character new viewers tend to meet first, and long-time fans have grown
up with.
