If you stopped watching EastEnders in the late 1990s, the biggest thing to know is
this: Cindy Beale is back, and she is played once again by **Michelle Collins**.

## The short version

Cindy was written out at the end of the 1990s, and the character was said to have
died. For years that was where her story stopped. In 2023 the show revealed she had
been alive all along, and brought her back to Albert Square — a genuinely rare case
of a long-dead soap character returning with the same actor who created the role.
