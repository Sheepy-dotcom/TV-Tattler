Every soap needs a character you can't quite look away from, and for Emmerdale
that's often Cain Dingle. Since arriving in 2000 — played throughout by **Jeff
Hordley** — he's been the village's most magnetic troublemaker, and its most
reliable protector when the Dingles are threatened.

See his [reference page](/characters/cain-dingle/) for the full portrayal record.
