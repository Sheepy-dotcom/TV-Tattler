Emmerdale began on 16 October 1972 under the name "Emmerdale Farm", a gentle drama
rooted in the farms of a fictional Yorkshire Dales village. Over time its focus
widened from the farm to the whole village, and in 1989 the title was shortened to
simply "Emmerdale".
