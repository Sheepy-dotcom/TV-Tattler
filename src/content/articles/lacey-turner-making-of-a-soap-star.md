Since arriving in EastEnders in 2004 as Stacey Slater, Lacey Turner has grown into
one of the most acclaimed performers in British soap, her name a fixture of the
genre's award ceremonies.

What has kept Stacey compelling across two decades is the range Turner brings —
comedy and tragedy, defiance and vulnerability, often within a single storyline.
