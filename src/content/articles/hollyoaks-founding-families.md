Hollyoaks has always been a young show, but a few families have given it ballast.
**Tony Hutchinson** (Nick Pickard) has been there since the very first episode in
1995, while **Cindy Cunningham** (Stephanie Waring) has carried one of the
village's founding family names since 2002. Around them, the **Osbornes** — Darren
(Ashley Taylor Dawson) and Nancy (Jessica Fox) — have become a modern anchor.

In a soap known for its fast turnover of youth, these are the constants.
