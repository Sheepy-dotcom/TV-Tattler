For all the affairs, betrayals and cliff-edge exits, the thing that keeps people
coming back to a soap is often much smaller: two characters who are simply a joy to
watch together. The great double act is a craft of its own, and Coronation Street
has long been its natural home.

Take **Tim Metcalfe** (Joe Duttine) and **Sally Metcalfe** (Sally Dynevor) — the
hapless cab driver and his house-proud, socially aspirational wife. Their marriage
is a decade-long comic engine: her grand plans, his gentle bafflement, and a
warmth underneath that makes the jokes land. Or **Gemma Winter** (Dolly-Rose
Campbell) and **Chesney Brown** (Sam Aston), whose motormouthed devotion to each
other and their family turns everyday chaos into some of the Street's funniest and
most tender moments.

What these pairings share is contrast held together by real affection — and the
sense that, whatever the week's drama, these two will face it as a team. That's
harder to write than any villain's scheme, and just as vital to why the form
endures.
