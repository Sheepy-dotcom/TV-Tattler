There has been a Barlow on Coronation Street since the very first episode in 1960.
**Ken Barlow** (William Roache) — schoolteacher, columnist and the show's moral
conscience — is the thread that runs the entire length of the programme, and the
family that gathers around him is its founding dynasty.

Ken's children carry the name in very different directions. **Peter Barlow**
(Chris Gascoyne) returned across two decades, his battles with addiction driving
some of the Street's most powerful drama. **Daniel Osbourne** (Rob Mallard), the
bookish youngest, brought a quieter, more literary sensibility. And **Tracy
Barlow** (Kate Ford) is the family's great schemer — sharp-tongued, unpredictable
and rarely out of a feud.

The tree keeps growing. **Adam Barlow** (Sam Robertson), Ken's sharp-suited
solicitor grandson, and **Amy Barlow** (Elle Mulvaney), Tracy's daughter with
Steve McDonald, are the generation carrying the dynasty forward.
