Ross Kemp arrived in EastEnders in 1990 as Grant Mitchell, the hotter-headed of
the two Mitchell brothers, and spent the decade as one of the soap's most
formidable figures. He bowed out as a regular in 1999 — but Grant's story didn't
end there.

In the years since, Kemp has returned for a series of high-profile guest stints,
each time pulling the Mitchell family's history back into the present. It's a
pattern EastEnders knows well: a departure that leaves the door deliberately ajar.
