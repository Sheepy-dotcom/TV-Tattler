Two brothers, one car lot, endless trouble: **Phil** (Steve McFadden) and
**Grant** (Ross Kemp) Mitchell landed in EastEnders in 1990 and quickly became the
show's most famous double act. Grant left as a regular in 1999; Phil never really
left at all.

Their pages are linked as family, so you can hop straight between
[Phil](/characters/phil-mitchell/) and [Grant](/characters/grant-mitchell/) — and
read each actor's tenure off the portrayal timeline.
