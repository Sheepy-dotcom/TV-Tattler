Every great continuing drama needs someone to fear — and someone to relish. The
soap villain is a particular art: menacing enough to drive a story, human enough
that the audience keeps watching. Emmerdale's **Kim Tate** (Claire King) is the
scheming matriarch in excelsis; EastEnders' **Phil Mitchell** (Steve McFadden) the
brooding hard man; Emmerdale's **Cain Dingle** (Jeff Hordley) the antihero you
root for despite yourself.

What they share is contradiction — capable of ruthlessness and, just often enough,
a flash of loyalty or regret. That tension is what keeps a good villain compelling
across decades.
