When EastEnders launched in 1985, Sharon Watts was there from the start, played by
Letitia Dean. Across the decades she has become the soap's definitive returner —
departing and coming back through some of its biggest eras, from the "Sharongate"
love triangle with the Mitchell brothers to her more recent chapters.

What sets Sharon apart from many long-running soap characters is continuity of
casting: through every exit and return, the role has stayed with Dean.
