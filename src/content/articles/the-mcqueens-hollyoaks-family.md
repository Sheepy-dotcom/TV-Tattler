Few families have given Hollyoaks as much energy as the McQueens. At the centre is
**Mercedes** (Jennifer Metcalfe), the bold, brassy heart of the clan since 2006,
with half-sister **Cleo** (Nadine Mulkerrin) among the family's most prominent
younger members.

Fiercely loyal to one another and rarely far from drama, the McQueens have been a
defining part of the village for the best part of two decades.
