No family has shaped EastEnders quite like the Mitchells. At its core are the
brothers — **Phil** (Steve McFadden), the linchpin of Albert Square since 1990,
and **Grant** (Ross Kemp), the harder, hotter-headed half of the pair. Around them
orbit their put-upon cousin **Billy** (Perry Fenwick), his one-time wife **Honey**
(Emma Barton), and **Sharon** (Letitia Dean), forever entwined with the brothers'
history.

Together they have carried a remarkable share of the show's biggest stories — a
dynasty built on loyalty, grudges and the Queen Vic.
