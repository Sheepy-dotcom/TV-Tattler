Few families have powered as many Coronation Street storylines as the Platts. At
the top sits **Audrey Roberts** (Sue Nicholls), the salon-owning matriarch on the
cobbles since 1979. Her daughter **Gail** (Helen Worth) has survived more marriages
and calamities than most, and Gail's son **David** (Jack P. Shepherd) has grown
from troubled teenager into one of the Street's most complicated leading men.

Three generations, three long-serving actors — the Platts are a case study in how
soap families accumulate history until every new story carries the weight of the
old ones.
