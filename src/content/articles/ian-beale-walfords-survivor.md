If you want a single character to explain what EastEnders is, it is hard to do
better than Ian Beale. He has been there since the first episode in February 1985,
played the whole way through by **Adam Woodyatt** — the only actor ever to take the
role.

## The constant of Albert Square

Market trader, café owner, chip-shop proprietor, funeral director: Ian has tried
nearly every trade Walford offers, usually badly, occasionally brilliantly. His
marriages — most famously to Cindy — have supplied some of the show's biggest
plots.

## Why he matters to the archive

A character with one actor across four decades is a gift for reference work: the
portrayal record is a single, unbroken line. See his character page for the
timeline, his family links, and everything we cover about the Beale family.
