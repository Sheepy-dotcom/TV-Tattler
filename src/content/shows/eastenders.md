EastEnders first aired on 19 February 1985 and is built around the residents of
Albert Square, a Victorian square in the fictional borough of Walford, E20. Its
long-running institutions — the Queen Victoria pub, the market, the café and the
launderette — anchor storylines that have run for decades.
